# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Developers, technical recruiters, and collaborators who land on the site to evaluate Theo Psallidas as a software engineer. Context: desktop-primary, short dwell time, high technical literacy. They're not being guided — they're exploring.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that puts Theo's experience first: a recruiter sees the current role, the agentic-platform work (LangGraph, RAG, OpenFGA), and education without typing anything. Success looks like: a visitor leaves with a clear picture of who Theo is as an engineer and can download the CV or email within seconds.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, technical, confident. The portfolio speaks for itself — no hype, no filler, no apology.

## Anti-references

- Generic developer portfolio templates (hero + cards + skills bars)
- Over-animated or flashy UIs (scroll-jacking, particle explosions, excessive motion)
- Corporate / enterprise formality (LinkedIn-style, navy-and-teal professionalism)
- Hacker aesthetic clichés (green-on-black Matrix ripoffs, skull icons, edgy overdesign)

## Design Principles

1. **Craft over decoration** — every visual element earns its place through function. If it doesn't communicate something, it doesn't exist.
2. **Honest surfaces** — no fake status, dead controls, or advertised features that do not exist. If it looks interactive, it works.
3. **Confident restraint** — the design trusts the user. No hand-holding, no tooltips explaining the obvious, no animations that narrate what the user can already see.
4. **Precision as reputation** — inconsistent spacing, mismatched tokens, or sloppy alignment are credibility damage. A software engineer's portfolio reflects their standards.
5. **Depth over noise** — delight comes from unexpected detail (annotated source lines, `= note:` explanations, keyboard jumps between roles), not motion or decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AAA where achievable. Full keyboard navigation, sufficient contrast throughout, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected. Monospace type stack already aids readability. No motion-critical interactions.
